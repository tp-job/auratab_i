# AuraTab Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-18_

AuraTab is a browser extension that replaces the New Tab page with a dashboard of your most visited sites and bookmarks.

## What AuraTab accesses

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most visited sites (`topSites`) | Shows the "Most visited" tiles | Read in your browser only; never stored or sent anywhere |
| Bookmarks (`bookmarks`) | Shows your bookmarks grouped by folder | Read in your browser only; never modified, stored or sent anywhere |
| Site icons (`favicon`) | Shows each site's icon from the browser's own local icon cache | Stays in your browser |
| Settings and open counts (`storage`) | Saves your preferences, hidden sites, and how many times you opened each link from AuraTab (for the optional "Most opened first" order) | Saved only in the browser's local extension storage on this device |
| Search text (`search`) | When you press Enter and nothing matches, your text is passed to **your browser's default search engine**, the same as typing it in the address bar | Handled by your browser and chosen search engine, never by AuraTab |

## What AuraTab does not do

- It makes no network requests. Its Content Security Policy sets `connect-src 'none'`, which blocks them.
- It has no analytics, tracking, ads or remote code.
- It does not sell, transfer or share any data with third parties.
- It does not read the content of the pages you visit.

## Your control

- **Settings → Your data → Clear open counts** deletes the usage statistics.
- **Settings → Your data → Restore hidden sites** clears the hidden-sites list.
- Uninstalling AuraTab deletes all of its stored data.
